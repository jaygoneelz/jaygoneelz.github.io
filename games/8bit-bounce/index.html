How to play

  • WASD / arrows move relative to the camera. Space jumps. Jump from near the edge toward the next numbered platform.
  • Drag the scene or use Q/E to rotate your view. The blue marble is you; the red cubes chase and attack.
  • Gold platforms move. Ride them for another route upward. Every third platform saves a checkpoint.
  • Yellow pickup: higher jumps for five seconds. Pink pickup: recover 20 health.
  • Enter the summit ring or survive five minutes to win. Falling off the island costs 25 health and returns you to your checkpoint.
  • P / Escape pauses, M toggles sound, B shows collision boxes. Touch controls appear on phones.
