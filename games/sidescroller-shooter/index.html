MISSION BRIEFING

How to play

  • Move with WASD / arrows, or the touch pad. Hold Space or FIRE to shoot.
  • Survive all three sector countdowns. You have three lives.
  • Enemies: white scouts fly straight, orange weavers patrol, purple hunters aim then charge.
  • Pickups: R rapid fire, S shield, + repair. Timed effects last six seconds.
  • P pauses, M toggles sound, F3 shows hitboxes. Escape pauses or returns.
